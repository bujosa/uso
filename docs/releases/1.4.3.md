# Uso 1.4.3

The panel closes when you click anything else, and the first row no longer opens highlighted.

## Install

Paste this in Terminal. It picks the build for your chip, checks the checksum and signature, installs Uso in `~/Applications`, and opens it without the Privacy & Security step:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bujosa/uso/main/scripts/get.sh | bash
```

Or pick the zip for your Mac. Apple menu > About This Mac shows a Chip (Apple M…) or a Processor (Intel).

- **Apple silicon:** `Uso-1.4.3-macOS-apple-silicon.zip`
- **Intel:** `Uso-1.4.3-macOS-intel.zip`

Unzip it and move `Uso.app` to Applications. Quit the previous copy before replacing it. Open Uso and look in the menu bar.

macOS 14 or later. The app is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks its first launch, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway; on macOS 14 you can instead right-click the app and choose Open. Each zip has a `.sha256` checksum.

## Changes

- **The panel closes on any outside click.** Uso closed the panel only when it lost keyboard focus. Another app's menu bar item opens without taking focus, so the panel stayed open. A click outside Uso, another menu bar item included, or another app coming forward, now closes it. Clicking Uso's own item still opens and closes it.
- **No highlighted first row.** With macOS keyboard navigation on (System Settings > Keyboard), the first row got keyboard focus on open and macOS drew its blue focus ring, clipped to two lines across the row. The panel now opens with nothing focused, and rows no longer draw the system ring. Tab still moves focus into the panel.
- **No leftover hover.** A row hovered when the panel closed kept its hover fill the next time it opened. Rows now reset it on every open.
- Escape closes the panel even when nothing in it has focus.

## Validation

- `swift test`: 62 tests in 6 suites passed. `swift format lint --strict` on the changed files passed.
- Live, macOS 26 with keyboard navigation on, driving clicks with `cliclick` and checking Uso's on-screen windows:
  - Clicking Uso's item four times opened, closed, opened, closed.
  - Clicking another menu bar item (Gaisan) closed the panel.
  - Clicking another app's window closed the panel.
  - Escape closed the panel, before and after pressing Tab.
  - Screenshots on open show no focus ring on the first row.
- Not checked: how a row looks once Tab focuses it.

## Compatibility

No migration is required. Uso watches mouse clicks outside the app only while the panel is open, and needs no new permission for that.
