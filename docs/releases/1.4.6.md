# Uso 1.4.6

Claude's Week bar now shows how much of it went to Fable.

## Install

Paste this in Terminal. It picks the build for your chip, checks the checksum and signature, installs Uso in `~/Applications`, and opens it without the Privacy & Security step:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bujosa/uso/main/scripts/get.sh | bash
```

Or pick the zip for your Mac. Apple menu > About This Mac shows a Chip (Apple M…) or a Processor (Intel).

- **Apple silicon:** `Uso-1.4.6-macOS-apple-silicon.zip`
- **Intel:** `Uso-1.4.6-macOS-intel.zip`

Unzip it and move `Uso.app` to Applications. Quit the previous copy before replacing it. Open Uso and look in the menu bar.

macOS 14 or later. The app is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks its first launch, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway; on macOS 14 you can instead right-click the app and choose Open. Each zip has a `.sha256` checksum.

## Changes

- **Fable inside the week.** Anthropic reports how much of the shared week a model with its own allowance used, and how far it may go. Claude's Week bar paints that part in a lighter tone at its start, marks the model's cap with a tick, and adds a legend such as "Fable 1% of week · up to 50%". It shows on the panel row and in the detail view.
- Nothing changes when Anthropic sends no share. The model's own week meter (Fable week) is still listed in the detail and can still be picked for the row.

## Validation

- `swift test`: 68 tests in 6 suites passed, adding share parsing from `weekly_scoped_shares` (named through `limit_index`) and tolerance for missing index, missing usage, and a 100% cap.
- `swift format lint --strict` on Sources, Tests, and `Package.swift` passed.
- Checked against this Mac's live Claude response (Ultra): week 13%, Fable 1% of the week with a 50% allowance.
- The bar and legend were rendered offscreen at 1% and 30% to check the colors; the full panel wasn't opened on screen.

## Compatibility

No migration is required.
