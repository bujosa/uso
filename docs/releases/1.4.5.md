# Uso 1.4.5

Pick which meters each row shows when the panel opens, including Antigravity's Claude & GPT pool.

## Install

Paste this in Terminal. It picks the build for your chip, checks the checksum and signature, installs Uso in `~/Applications`, and opens it without the Privacy & Security step:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bujosa/uso/main/scripts/get.sh | bash
```

Or pick the zip for your Mac. Apple menu > About This Mac shows a Chip (Apple M…) or a Processor (Intel).

- **Apple silicon:** `Uso-1.4.5-macOS-apple-silicon.zip`
- **Intel:** `Uso-1.4.5-macOS-intel.zip`

Unzip it and move `Uso.app` to Applications. Quit the previous copy before replacing it. Open Uso and look in the menu bar.

macOS 14 or later. The app is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks its first launch, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway; on macOS 14 you can instead right-click the app and choose Open. Each zip has a `.sha256` checksum.

## Changes

- **Panel row shows.** In Settings > Accounts & plans, every source with more than one meter has a Panel row shows menu. Tick the meters you want on its row: only the week, or for Gemini, Antigravity's Claude & GPT 5h or week next to (or instead of) Gemini's own. The first pick sets the row's percentage.
- The last meter can't be unticked, and Use default goes back to the standard meters. A pick that no longer matches anything the service sends falls back to the defaults instead of an empty row.
- Choices are saved on this Mac. The detail view still lists every meter, and the menu bar keeps its own rotation.
